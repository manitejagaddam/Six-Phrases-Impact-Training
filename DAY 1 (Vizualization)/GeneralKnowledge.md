# 🌗 Equinox

- The **Equinox** is a celestial phenomenon that occurs **twice a year** — around **March 20th** and **September 23rd**.
- On these two days, the **duration of day and night is nearly equal** all over the world.
- This happens because the **Earth's axis is tilted**, but during an equinox, the **tilt is neither away from nor toward the Sun**.
- Equinox marks the:
  - 🌸 **Vernal Equinox** – Beginning of Spring (around March)
  - 🍂 **Autumnal Equinox** – Beginning of Autumn (around September)
- The Earth completes one revolution around the Sun in **approximately 365.242375 days**, which is:
  - **365 days, 5 hours, 48 minutes, and 46 seconds**
- This fractional day leads to the necessity of a **leap year every 4 years** to keep our calendar aligned with the seasons.

# 📱 Why Is It Called a Cell Phone?

- The term **"cell phone"** is derived from the **cellular network architecture** used to provide mobile communication.
- The coverage area is divided into smaller sections called **cells**, which are typically shaped like a **hexagon** to create a **honeycomb pattern**.
- Each **cell** contains a **base station (transceiver/tower)** that communicates with mobile devices within that zone.
- As a user moves from one cell to another, the mobile phone automatically connects to the **nearest tower** in the new cell — this process is known as **handoff**.
- This design allows:
  - Efficient **frequency reuse** without interference.
  - **Seamless connectivity** while on the move.
  - Support for **millions of simultaneous users** across the network.
- The cell-based structure was a **game-changer in wireless communication**, forming the backbone of modern mobile networks like **4G, 5G**, etc.
