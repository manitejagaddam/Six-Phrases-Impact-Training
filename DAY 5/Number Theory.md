# NUMBER THEORY :
- In Decimals Left Shifting is multiplying it by 10
- In Binary Left Shifting is multiplying it by 2
- In Octals Left Shifting is multiplying it by 8
- In Hexa Decimals Left Shifting is multiplying it by 16

## Overall
- Left Shifting of a number is multiplication of the same number by their own bases
- Right Shifting of a number is Division of the same number by their own bases
